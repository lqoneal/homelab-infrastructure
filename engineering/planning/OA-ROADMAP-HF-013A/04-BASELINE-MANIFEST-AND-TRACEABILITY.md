## Conformance obligations

Every future implementation package shall identify:

1. baseline identifier and immutable locator;
2. consumed authoritative metadata and interface contracts;
3. applicable subsystem work package;
4. applicable conformance fixtures and expected evidence;
5. qualification package and acceptance criteria; and
6. a separate implementation authorization.

No generated artifact, runtime projection, or planning document may claim to
supersede the registry record or the immutable baseline locator.
