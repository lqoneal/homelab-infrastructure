# Zeus Autonomous Correction Policy

Zeus automatically diagnoses and repairs missing, partial, stale, equivalent, and interrupted derived state when Stage 1 authority and identity bindings prove one lawful result. The repair plan is deterministic, transaction-scoped, journaled, and replayable.

Zeus stops fail-closed for conflicting immutable package, source, authority, transaction, provider, dispatch, or mission identities; ambiguous ownership; invalid lineage; unsynchronized protected state; or non-atomic persistence. Internal inconsistency alone never creates a new authority decision.
