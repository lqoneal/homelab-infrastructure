# ZDCL Autonomous Mission Delivery Roadmap

## Revision

1.0 — prepublication candidate

## Advancement

The ZDCL roadmap now treats autonomous mission delivery as the next capability
layer after receipt-backed Stage 1 runtime reconciliation. The implementation
sequence is: lifecycle ledger, shared reconciliation, autonomous diagnosis,
bounded corrective execution, qualification, publication orchestration,
approval-aware publication, EOS synchronization, and closeout.

Each step must preserve the Operational Alpha authority chain and may advance
only after disposable qualification, controlled-document validation, Registry
validation, platform validation, and governed publication.
