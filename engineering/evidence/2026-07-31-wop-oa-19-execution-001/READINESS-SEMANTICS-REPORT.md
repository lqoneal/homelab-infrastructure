# OA-19 Readiness Semantics Report

Readiness evaluates `capability_prerequisites` as pre-existing operational
requirements. `capability_outcomes` identifies the capability delivered by
the mission and must not be evaluated as an entry prerequisite.

Before reconciliation, readiness reported CAP-018 missing because the Mission
Knowledge Model supplied the outcome capability in the prerequisite field.
After reconciliation, OA-19 readiness evaluates CAP-017, the OA-18 outcome,
and classifies the mission as eligible. CAP-018 is reported separately as a
missing outcome, remaining Planned/UNAVAILABLE until OA-19 is independently
qualified. It is not an entry blocker.

The OA-19 objective and acceptance criteria remain unchanged.
