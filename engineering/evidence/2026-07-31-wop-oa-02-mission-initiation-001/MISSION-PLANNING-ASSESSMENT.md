mission executes without valid, current, discoverable authority. Sources:

`OA-02_AND_LATER=INELIGIBLE`, and `docs/project/PROJ-0001-PROJECT_STATE.md`
states that OA-02 requires separately authorized admission and must not be
inferred from OA-01 closeout.

## Conclusion

objective. Planning identity is not sufficient to create an Authority Record,
Operational Gate Plan, Activation Record, or runtime implementation WOP.
