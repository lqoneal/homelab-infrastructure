5. `CAGF-02` — cross-source identity/dependency validation and provenance manifests.
6. `EPE-02` — mission transaction and resumable commit boundary.
7. `EPE-03` — append-only engineering execution ledger and projections.
8. `ZDCL-03` / `CAGF-03` — controlled workspaces, approvals, publication integration, and broader generated projections.
9. `EPE-04` — dependency-aware validation.
10. `EPE-05` — structured recommendations and qualified work generation.

Each item is a planning backlog entry only. A future WOP must resolve its objective and authorize its exact capability before implementation.
