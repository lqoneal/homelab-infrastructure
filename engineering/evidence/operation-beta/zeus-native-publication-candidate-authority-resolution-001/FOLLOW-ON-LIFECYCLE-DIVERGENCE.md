# Follow-on lifecycle divergence

The operator-provided qualification record describes lower-level managed
runtime facts indicating provider selection, dispatch, provider session,
provider invocation, execution-session creation, and execution start, while a
higher-level mission aggregate can still expose the earlier dispatch-boundary
state. It also records `mission_work_actually_occurred=NO` and
`repository_work_actually_occurred=NO`.

This is not repaired by candidate authority resolution. It is recorded as:

`FOLLOW_ON_REQUIRED=LIFECYCLE_AGGREGATE_PROJECTION_AND_MANAGED_RUNTIME_DIVERGENCE_RECONCILIATION`

No managed Codex process was terminated or superseded by this corrective. No
controlled mission work, CAGF-01 work, publication, push, or EOS synchronization
was performed.
