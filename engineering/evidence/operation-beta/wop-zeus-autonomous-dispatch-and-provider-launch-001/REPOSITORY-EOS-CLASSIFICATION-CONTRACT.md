# Repository–EOS Classification Contract

The canonical classifier is `scripts/lib/eos/validation_lifecycle.py`. Stage 2 invokes it through `state_sync.py`; Stage 4 shell checks consume its expected published baseline. This prevents independent interpretations of candidate divergence. `EOS_STALE`, `OUTDATED_CANDIDATE`, `DIVERGENT_CANDIDATE`, `DIRTY`, `DETACHED`, `UNRELATED_BRANCH`, and `AMBIGUOUS` remain fail-closed.
