# Dispatch Contract

Canonical command: scripts/zeus dispatch create <MISSION_ID> --json.

It consumes live provider selection and binds Mission/WOP, repository,
submission/admission/bootstrap, provider-selection, and lineage identity. It
creates exactly six immutable current dispatch artifacts and stops at provider
session readiness.

DISPATCH_ID=DISPATCH-18865edc-5878-57c0-ae43-c697f01e3325
DISPATCH_STATE=READY_FOR_PROVIDER_SESSION
PROVIDER_ID=zeus-local-loneal-01
DISPATCH_RECEIPT_PRESENT=YES
DISPATCH_REPLAY=IDEMPOTENT
DUPLICATE_DISPATCH_CREATED=NO
PROVIDER_INVOCATION=NO
EXECUTION=NO

Historical and cross-mission artifacts were preserved and excluded.
