# Root Cause Analysis

The candidate resolver had Mission/WOP and qualification authority but no
source-level grouping authority. It therefore unioned independently qualified
claims for shared paths and could not prove that the claims belonged to one
publication boundary.

The corrective adds a durable repository-bound Publication Cohort. Cohort
membership names qualified source identities and dependencies; paths continue
to derive from member manifests. The superseded transaction remains immutable.
