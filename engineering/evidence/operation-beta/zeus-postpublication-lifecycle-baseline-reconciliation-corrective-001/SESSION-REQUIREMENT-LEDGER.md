## Chronology and supersession

The early CAGF and activation prompts remain recorded because they are part of
the session history. Later operator boundaries explicitly deferred CAGF-01 and
prohibited provider/execution progression during this corrective; therefore
R001 and R013 are retained as historical requirements and linked as
`SUPERSEDED`, not silently deleted.

The original lifecycle state requirements that stopped at
`ADMISSION_REQUESTED` were superseded by the later operator-authorized
admission/bootstrap/P3/P4 work, but the current corrective still prohibits any
provider or execution transition. The current target is the postpublication
pre-provider boundary.
