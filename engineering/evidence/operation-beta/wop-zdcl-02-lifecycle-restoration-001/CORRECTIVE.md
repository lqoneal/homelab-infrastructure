# Corrective

`_resolve_baseline_transition` now:

- validates the publication receipt against its own immutable resulting baseline;
- verifies that baseline is an ancestor of current synchronized `main`;
- records the intervening paths;
- accepts only documentation/evidence-only descendants;
- rejects implementation or other unauthorized descendants fail-closed;
- binds the actual current HEAD as the recovery baseline.

The operator interface and authority model are unchanged. No receipt or
authority artifact is generated by the corrective.
