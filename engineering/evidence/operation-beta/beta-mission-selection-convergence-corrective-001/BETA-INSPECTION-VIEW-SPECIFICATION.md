# Beta Inspection View Specification

For `CAGF-01`, the supported read-only views are:

- `zeus mission authority CAGF-01`: Governance authority, source bindings,
  protected baselines, and selection-only boundary;
- `zeus mission contract CAGF-01`: Beta roadmap selection contract, scope,
  dependencies, lifecycle, classification, and readiness;
- `zeus mission snapshot CAGF-01`: state projection, canonical next action,
  and selection binding.

These are inspection projections only. They do not create or require a Mission
Contract, admission, execution, or lifecycle transition.
