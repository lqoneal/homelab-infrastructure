# Compatibility and Migration

`governance_authority`, `governance_submission`, `submitted_by`, and
`submitter_identity` remain accepted where present. They are classified as
legacy-compatible metadata or provenance and do not establish execution,
production, publication, acceptance, or closeout authority. They are no
longer mandatory merely to cross the canonical submission boundary.

Existing package identities and canonical CAGF source/package artifacts were
not modified.
