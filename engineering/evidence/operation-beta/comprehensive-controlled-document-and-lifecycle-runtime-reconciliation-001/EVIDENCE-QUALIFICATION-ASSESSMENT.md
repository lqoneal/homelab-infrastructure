# Evidence and Qualification Assessment

Evidence capture and qualification modules have focused component coverage, including requirement-level qualification behavior. The architecture requires independent evidence manifests, traceability, freshness, and qualification.

What is not proven is the non-circular chain from actual mission-work receipt through evidence production, independent qualification, publication readiness, and terminal closeout for the target mission. A future corrective must prevent a self-declared execution-success claim from being the sole input to qualification.
