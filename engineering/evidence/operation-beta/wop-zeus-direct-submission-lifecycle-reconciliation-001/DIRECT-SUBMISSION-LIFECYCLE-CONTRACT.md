# Direct Submission Lifecycle Contract

`zeus submit <WOP>` creates the Stage 1 transaction. It may consume an unpublished package. It establishes authority from Engineering Governance’s submission decision, admission identity, execution identity, and receipt lineage. It does not require merge, publication, EOS synchronization, provider selection, or provider launch at the submission boundary. Zeus owns subsequent admission, execution, recovery, qualification, publication approval, publication, synchronization, and closeout.
