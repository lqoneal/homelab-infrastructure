# Canonical Qualification Contract

The canonical resolver is `scripts.lib.emp.qualification_contract`. It is read-only, digest-bound, and the sole owner of publication readiness.

Required fields are `qualification_state`, `publication_state`, evidence completeness, platform, validation, registry, controlled-document, lifecycle, authority, EOS, provenance, typed blockers, and next authorized action. Missing or contradictory evidence resolves fail-closed.
