# EOS Synchronization Report

## Intended projection

Following publication, EOS will synchronize the published project-state,
implementation-status, dispatcher-capability, and runtime-state projections.
The synchronization receipt and validation result are recorded by the
post-publication amendment to this report.

## Lifecycle protection

This WOP does not change the authoritative OA lifecycle. OA-01 remains
`READY / NOT_STARTED`; no Authority Record, Operational Gate Plan, activation,
admission, or mission execution is created by synchronization.
