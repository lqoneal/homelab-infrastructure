# Dispatcher Simplification Publication Report

## Scope

This publication makes the convergence receipt the sole Operational Alpha
dispatcher-admission predicate. It publishes the qualified routing change,
controlled-document reconciliation, EMM digest reconciliation, regression
coverage, and this evidence package.

## Outcome

The Progressive PMCT, legacy authority-publication, production-dispatcher, and
legacy-agent paths remain preserved as compatibility or historical tooling.
They cannot grant, deny, or modify an Operational Alpha admission or execution
decision. No new foundational architecture was introduced.

## Publication identity

The immutable commit identity and remote publication receipt are recorded by
the post-publication amendment to this report.
