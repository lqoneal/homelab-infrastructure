# Controlled Documentation Reconciliation

The Engineering Platform state standard now records OA-24's projection
performance principle: human-readable views remain read-only projections of
authoritative state, and only context-local reuse is permitted without a new
freshness authority. The OA-24 implementation, gate, PMCT, roadmap, MKM,
Capability Registry, and EMM retain identical objective, prerequisite, and
outcome semantics.

ZDCL, CAGF, EMP, EENS, distributed execution, and persistent presentation
caching remain deferred and were not implemented.
