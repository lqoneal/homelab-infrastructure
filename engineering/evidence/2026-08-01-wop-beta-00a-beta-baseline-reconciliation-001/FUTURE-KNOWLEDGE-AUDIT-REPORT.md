# Future Knowledge Audit Report

The audit distinguishes knowledge from authority. Canonical and normative
records remain controlled sources. Runtime state, projections, and historical
evidence are not authority. Planning artifacts describe intended work but do
not authorize it.

The strongest CAGF candidates are bounded projections: roadmap/readiness,
blockers/prerequisites, qualified gate manifests, controller views, and
provenance indexes. Mission objectives, capability identity/state, approvals,
publication decisions, lifecycle transitions, and EOS runtime state must not be
generated as authority. Standards and recommendations may gain consistency or
index generation only after their ownership and qualification contracts exist.

This audit is architectural analysis only. It adds no generator, runtime
behavior, capability, or lifecycle state.
