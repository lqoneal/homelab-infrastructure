# CAGF Readiness Assessment

## Result

CAGF is ready for a separately authorized source-contract foundation mission,
but not for broad artifact generation. Canonical owners are known and the
candidate projection families are bounded. Missing prerequisites are stable
schemas, digest inputs, cross-source identity validation, cycle detection,
byte-stability qualification, and a publication manifest.

## Readiness boundary

The first CAGF mission should qualify one projection family end to end before
expanding to gates, controllers, and all operational metadata. Until then,
existing manually maintained canonical records and validated projections remain
in force. No generation capability is implemented by BETA-00A.
