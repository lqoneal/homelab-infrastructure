# CHAR-0001 v1.1 Constitutional Reconciliation Report

Date: 2026-07-22  
Scope: constitutional consistency review and minimum subordinate-document reconciliation  
Status: working-tree reconciliation evidence; controlled publication and activation remain subject to the existing governance lifecycle

## Executive Result

The review identified one constitutional circular dependency and three related ambiguities. The working-tree revisions remove the requirement for a repository-originated Authorization Kernel to precede a Governance-issued Engineering Handoff, recognize the Charter-defined transitional initiation effect, and preserve every downstream EWO, lifecycle, publication, qualification, evidence, traceability, and repository boundary control.

No document produced by this review claims to approve itself, activate a lifecycle transition, authorize EWO execution, or complete controlled publication.

## Documents Reviewed

The complete normative review included CHAR-0001, GEN-0001, POL-0001, STD-0000, STD-0001, STD-0002, STD-0003, STD-0004, PROC-0001 through PROC-0007, EDR-0002, EDR-0003, SPEC-0001, SPEC-0006, SPEC-0008, TPL-0001, TPL-0002, and DOC-0001. Repository-wide term discovery also covered active project, service, specification, resolution, template, roadmap, historical work-order, milestone, planning, evidence, and manual-workflow records.

Historical work orders, resolutions, milestones, evidence, planning records, and archived/manual workflow copies were reviewed as historical evidence and were not rewritten to express current normative semantics.

## Constitutional Conflicts and Resolutions

| ID | Conflict or ambiguity | Resolution | Control preservation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CR-01 | PROC-0004 required an Authorization Kernel before Handoff construction, making repository-controlled input a prerequisite to the Charter-recognized Handoff. | The Governance-issued Handoff is the transitional initiating input; a Kernel is optional structured content embedded in or referenced by it. | EWO construction, Governance review, approval, activation, and authority-preservation validation remain mandatory. |
| CR-02 | POL-0001 classified all Handoffs as historical or mission-input artifacts without recognizing CHAR-0001 v1.1. | Added the narrow Charter-defined transitional exception and made Charter precedence explicit. | Handoffs cannot override repository lifecycle behavior or confer execution authority. |
| CR-03 | PROC-0005 required explicit preparation authority but did not state whether a transitional Handoff could satisfy initiation. | A Governance-issued Handoff may satisfy preparation/workflow initiation only when scope is explicit. | Approval, lifecycle transition, publication, commit, push, tag, qualification, and implementation authority remain separate. |
| CR-04 | GEN-0001's prohibition on repeating bootstrap could be read to prohibit the new transitional mechanism. | Distinguished temporary Handoff initiation from the one-time Genesis Bootstrap. | Genesis remains unique and closed; ordinary downstream controls remain intact. |

## Validation

### Governance hierarchy

Pass. Every reconciled path begins with the Engineering Organization, Engineering Governance, and CHAR-0001. No repository record is described as originating constitutional authority.

### Lifecycle controls

Pass. The Handoff initiates processing only. An Active EWO remains the ordinary bounded execution authorization. Review, approval, activation, publication, qualification, evidence, traceability, stop conditions, and repository boundary controls remain explicit.

### Circular-dependency elimination

Pass for the identified normative cycle. PROC-0004 no longer requires a separately repository-originated Kernel before recognizing the Governance-issued Handoff. The optional Kernel structures Handoff content but cannot become a competing authority source.

### Charter consistency

Pass at content level for the reviewed normative set. The reconciled texts recognize the temporary rule, preserve Charter precedence, distinguish initiation from execution, and retain automatic termination when the Charter's automated-Handoff condition is met.

### Repository integrity

Historical records and unrelated worktree content were not modified. Controlled publication, versioning, approval metadata, index synchronization, immutable persistence, and activation have not been asserted by this working-tree report and remain required follow-on lifecycle work.

### Tool evidence

- Both failures are pre-existing DOC-0001 relationship issues unrelated to this reconciliation: unrecognized relationship type `discovers` and unresolved target `EENS-OPERATIONAL-ALPHA`.
- A scoped normative conflict scan found no remaining statement that classifies every Handoff as historical/non-authoritative, requires a separate Kernel before Handoff processing, or requires future initiation to operate without the Charter's transitional Handoff rule.

## Unresolved Governance Issues

The revised controlled documents still require ordinary review, revision metadata, approval, publication, index synchronization, persistence evidence, and any required qualification before the repository may claim an activated reconciled baseline.
