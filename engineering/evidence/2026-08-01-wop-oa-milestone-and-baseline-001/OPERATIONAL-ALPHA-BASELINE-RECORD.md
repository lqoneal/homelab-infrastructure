# Operational Alpha Baseline Record

The authoritative frozen baseline is the commit resolved by the annotated Git
tag `OA-v1.0.0` in `lqoneal/homelab-infrastructure`, branch `main`.

The baseline contains OA-01 through OA-30 completed and CAP-001 through
CAP-030 operational, available, and passing. It includes the published
Engineering Platform Evolution Phase 1 architecture and roadmap, while ZDCL,
CAGF, and all other post-Operational Alpha implementations remain deferred.

The tag target, canonical HEAD, and `origin/main` are required to be identical
and are recorded in the tag publication report after tag publication.
