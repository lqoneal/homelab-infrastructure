# Reconciliation Contract

After every gate compare affected source and derived records by identity and
digest. Consider Project State, Work Registry, mission/gate state, WOP state,
PMCT, EOS source and projections, EENS references, authority indices, Mission
Contract lifecycle, repository topology, resume state, verification register,
and completion reports.

Repository-authored records are reconciled only in the canonical tree.
External operational facts enter through typed receipts with provenance, not
copied files. No reconciliation result creates upstream authority.
