# ZDCL-01 Foundation Roadmap

Mission: `ZDCL-01`

The increment establishes only the bounded native session foundation:

1. Session identity and immutable execution identity.
2. Session classification and effect profile.
3. Canonical repository identity verification.
4. Qualified development-baseline verification.
5. Evidence capture and interruption-safe closeout.

Prerequisite: `BETA-00`, satisfied by the published Operation Beta assessment,
reconciliation, and planning baseline.

Successor increments remain outside this package. The published roadmap remains
the authority for their sequencing.
