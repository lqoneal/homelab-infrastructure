# Historical OA Package Supersedence

`GH-ZEUS-OA-CERTIFICATION-001` is suspended and superseded for future OA
execution because it does not contain complete gate engineering objectives and
verification contracts. Its files and accepted admission record remain
unchanged historical evidence. Its OA gates shall not be executed.

The old package conferred no status on `GH-ZEUS-OA-PROGRESSIVE-001`. The new
OA-01 through OA-30 are all unstarted and require independent verification and
acceptance under `ZEUS-OA-GATE-SPEC-002`. Historical PMCT runs and prior Zeus
implementation may be cited only as supporting evidence after current-binding
validation.
