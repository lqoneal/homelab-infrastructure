# Zeus Operational Alpha Authority Ownership Specification

Date: 2026-07-26
Status: Authoritative production ownership model
Mission: ZEUS-P2-013

## Decision

Lawrence O'Neal is the sole designated human production authority for Zeus
Operational Alpha. The production principal is `loneal`, authenticated through
a Zeus CLI session.

This is the intentional Alpha architecture. Organizational labels used by
earlier designs—such as registries, controllers, providers, services,
committees, or separate operators—are functional domains, not additional human
owners or principals.

Zeus is the authority-resolution, validation, reconciliation, and execution
system. It does not possess independent authority, invent approvals,
self-authorize, or weaken a failed validation.
Every accepted command is an authenticated action of `loneal`, subject to the
repository's policy, lifecycle, signature, provenance, auditing, repository,
and runtime checks.

## CLI authority semantics

```text
Authenticated Operator (Lawrence O'Neal / loneal)
        |
        v
Zeus CLI
        |
        v
Repository Policy Enforcement
        |
        v
Authority Resolution Runtime
        |
        v
Mission Runtime
        |
        v
Execution
```

An authenticated Zeus command represents the complete human operational
authority needed to request that command. No secondary human approval is
required unless a repository policy explicitly defines one. The command still
fails unless all applicable policy and runtime prerequisites pass.

An approval record is therefore a signed, auditable declaration by `loneal`;
it is not a decision generated by Zeus and is not evidence of a second person.
The Authority Resolution Runtime verifies the approval record, scope,
lifecycle, ownership, repository baseline, authority graph, governing
baseline, identity, and provenance. It does not search for another human
approver.

## Authority restoration

Controlled documentation is the normal operational source of execution
authority and derives its authority ultimately from Lawrence O'Neal. Zeus
always attempts normal controlled-document resolution first.

Missing, stale, conflicting, incomplete, or invalid authority is reconciliation
work under SPEC-0011. Bootstrapping authorization permits reconciliation of
controlled documentation; it never bypasses controlled authority. After
reconciliation Zeus validates the repository, re-runs normal authority
resolution, and executes only under restored controlled-document authority.

## Authentication and publication

Production enrollment establishes one active owner:

- owner: `Lawrence O'Neal`
- principal: `loneal`
- key custody: Lawrence O'Neal

That enrolled principal may sign every record type, while each envelope
continues to identify its domain-specific record type. Detached SSH signatures,
canonical payload digests, deterministic envelope identifiers, create-only
staging, candidate reconstruction, readiness, explicit activation, receipts,
rollback, and revocation remain mandatory.

Possession of a CLI session alone does not bypass signature requirements. The
session must authenticate as `loneal`, and commands that consume signed records
must verify the enrolled key and repository-fixed trust policy.

## Future delegation

Future releases may assign different owners or principals to individual
authority domains without redesigning the runtime. Delegation changes only:

- authority records and ownership assignments;
- enrollment records and allowed signers;
- owner trust policy;
- publication envelopes and signatures.

Record types, Authority Resolution, staging, readiness, activation,
provenance, and audit architecture remain unchanged.

## Supersession

For production ownership, this specification supersedes earlier operational
documentation that describes eight independent organizational owners or a
separate human approval authority. Historical evidence remains accurate as a
record of the model and commissioning state observed when it was written.
