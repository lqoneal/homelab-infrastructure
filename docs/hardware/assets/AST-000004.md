# Private AI Assistant Role Assessment

The proposed Private AI Assistant platform role is **not assigned**. Existing
backup and media dependencies, dirty NTFS state, lack of a completed migration
and redundancy plan, and non-zero reallocated-sector history fail the
preservation, suitability, recovery, and operational-integration gates in
STD-0005. Reconsideration requires verified backup consolidation, separate
media migration, filesystem disposition, risk review of the reallocation
history, and an AI storage/backup architecture under separate authority.
