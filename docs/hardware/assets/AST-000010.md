## Preservation Assessment

- **Preserve and review:** `homedir.backup.tar.xz`; treat as potentially unique
  personal backup content until integrity and retention are separately assessed.
- **Archive or replace after verification:** the Ubuntu 22.04.5 installation
  tree; it is reproducible software but may retain recovery value.
- **Review:** autorun and icon metadata; no preservation value is asserted.
- **Repurpose only after migration and authorization:** the device may be
  considered for a future engineering role after the backup is protected and
  the exFAT dirty-volume and label anomalies are dispositioned.
- **Quarantine condition:** any new SMART, transport, read, or filesystem error
  requires immediate quarantine and evidence preservation.

No data was moved, copied, repaired, deleted, or modified during qualification.

## Engineering Role Assessment

**Assigned role:** Engineering preservation storage.

This role preserves the existing historical Ubuntu and home-directory backup;
it does not authorize temporary workspace, shared operational storage, repair,
writable mounting, migration, or reuse. The recommended Engineering
operational-storage role is deferred because `homedir.backup.tar.xz` may be
unique and the exFAT dirty-volume and label limitations remain unresolved.

Future migration shall first establish a governed backup destination, verify
the archive by recorded hash and readable test extraction to separate media,
retain the source through an approved retention interval, and only then seek
separate authority for filesystem disposition and reuse. Eventual reuse as
temporary or shared Engineering operational storage may be reconsidered after
those preservation, filesystem-health, redundancy, and integration gates pass.
