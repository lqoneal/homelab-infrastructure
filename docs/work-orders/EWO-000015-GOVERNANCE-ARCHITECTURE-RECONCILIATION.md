## Engineering Governance Intent

### Purpose

Authorize the Governance Architecture Reconciliation campaign as a bounded Governance Stabilization mission.

The campaign shall reconcile the foundational governance authority architecture through complete controlled revisions, incorporate applicable Engineering Governance decisions, validate each revised publication as a whole, and produce sufficient evidence and completion reporting for Engineering Governance acceptance.

### Engineering Governance Objectives

The implementation agent shall:

1. reconcile the governance architecture expressed by CHAR-0001, GEN-0001, POL-0001, and EDR-0002;
2. incorporate every previously approved but unimplemented Engineering Governance decision applicable to the active document unless Engineering Governance explicitly defers that decision;
3. incorporate newly approved Engineering Governance decisions applicable to the campaign;
4. preserve a clear, non-conflicting authority chain and governing relationships;
5. revise every authorized controlled document as a coherent, complete engineering publication;
6. perform whole-document validation for every revision;
7. conclude every document reconciliation with Sprint Conformance Verification;
8. collect objective, reproducible, attributable, and traceable engineering evidence; and
9. produce an Engineering Completion Report for Engineering Governance review.

### Mission Scope

This Work Order authorizes complete controlled revisions of:

* CHAR-0001 — Engineering Charter;
* GEN-0001 — Engineering Operating System Genesis Record;
* POL-0001 — Engineering Governance Policy;
* EDR-0002 — Engineering Authority Model; and
* subsequent governance records only when Engineering Governance explicitly authorizes the identified record during this campaign.

For each authorized record, the campaign may perform complete-document analysis, complete controlled revision, metadata and relationship reconciliation, decision incorporation, whole-document validation, Sprint Conformance Verification, engineering evidence collection, and engineering completion reporting.

An authorization for a subsequent governance record shall identify the record, the approved decision or reconciliation objective, and any additional constraints before modification begins. General campaign scope does not constitute authorization to modify an unidentified record.

### Mission Constraints

The implementation agent shall:

* review every controlled document in its entirety before analyzing or revising it;
* treat every revision as one complete publication;
* perform no piecemeal editing;
* preserve historical integrity and traceability;
* execute authorized document reconciliations sequentially unless Engineering Governance explicitly approves a different order;
* preserve unrelated working-tree modifications;
* stop after campaign completion reporting and await Engineering Governance acceptance.

The implementation agent shall not treat this Work Order's creation as authorization to begin reconciliation before EWO-000015 has been validated and accepted by Engineering Governance.

## Authority Model

### Operational Authority

Following Engineering Governance acceptance of this Work Order, the implementation agent is authorized to:

* verify the Work Order and governing baseline;
* inventory the repository, branch, commit, working tree, tools, and applicable controlled records;
* inspect repository-controlled records in their entirety;
* run non-destructive validation commands;
* collect engineering evidence; and
* prepare the required Engineering Evidence Package and Engineering Completion Report.

### Engineering Authority

Following Engineering Governance acceptance of this Work Order, the implementation agent is authorized to:

* create complete controlled revisions of CHAR-0001, GEN-0001, POL-0001, and EDR-0002;
* create complete controlled revisions of subsequent governance records explicitly authorized by Engineering Governance during the campaign;
* implement applicable previously approved but unimplemented Engineering Governance decisions unless explicitly deferred;
* implement newly approved Engineering Governance decisions within the authorized campaign scope;
* reconcile metadata, governing relationships, normative content, references, and revision history within each authorized complete publication;
* perform whole-document validation and Sprint Conformance Verification; and
* produce campaign evidence and completion reporting.

### Prohibited Activities

The implementation agent is not authorized to:

* begin governance reconciliation before EWO-000015 is validated and accepted by Engineering Governance;
* modify a controlled document before reviewing it in its entirety;
* perform piecemeal, fragment-only, or context-free controlled-document edits;
* modify any governance record not expressly identified by this Work Order or subsequently authorized by Engineering Governance during the campaign;
* infer, invent, approve, defer, or reject Engineering Governance decisions;
* modify unrelated controlled documents or unrelated working-tree changes;
* redesign governance beyond approved reconciliation decisions;
* commit, push, tag, publish, or alter remote repository state; or
* continue beyond a stop condition.

### Escalation Requirements

Engineering Governance authorization is required when:

* an applicable decision is ambiguous, conflicting, incomplete, or cannot be traced to authoritative approval;
* deferral of a previously approved applicable decision is proposed;
* a record outside the expressly authorized scope requires modification;
* governing records conflict or the authority hierarchy cannot be deterministically resolved;
* a complete controlled revision cannot be produced without affecting unrelated content;
* whole-document validation or Sprint Conformance Verification fails;
* repository integrity or deterministic execution cannot be maintained; or
* acceptance, lifecycle transition, publication, or follow-on execution is requested.

## Execution Overview

Engineering phases authorized by this Work Order after its validation and Engineering Governance acceptance are:

### Phase 0 — Execution Contract and Baseline Verification

Verify EWO-000015, the operational environment, repository integrity, governing baseline, approved decisions, authorized scope, and initial working-tree state.

### Phase 1 — Complete-Document Discovery and Decision Inventory

Read each applicable controlled document completely, establish its full governing context, and identify all applicable approved but unimplemented and newly approved Engineering Governance decisions.

### Phase 2 — Complete Controlled Revisions

Produce coherent complete controlled revisions of the authorized documents without piecemeal editing.

### Phase 3 — Whole-Document Validation and Sprint Conformance Verification

Validate each complete publication and verify campaign conformance after each reconciliation.

### Final Phase — Evidence and Completion Reporting

Collect campaign evidence, produce the Engineering Evidence Package and Engineering Completion Report, record findings, and stop for Engineering Governance review.

## Success Criteria

### Mission Success

The mission succeeds when every document authorized and undertaken during the campaign has been reconciled as a complete controlled publication, every applicable approved decision has been implemented or explicitly deferred by Engineering Governance, all whole-document validation and Sprint Conformance Verification results pass, and the execution record is sufficient for Engineering Governance acceptance.

### Definition of Done

This Work Order is complete when:

* CHAR-0001, GEN-0001, POL-0001, and EDR-0002 have each received an authorized complete controlled revision;
* every subsequently authorized governance record undertaken in the campaign has received an authorized complete controlled revision;
* every applicable previously approved but unimplemented decision has been incorporated unless explicitly deferred by Engineering Governance;
* every applicable newly approved decision has been incorporated;
* metadata, governing relationships, repository references, normative content, and revision history are internally consistent;
* every revised record passes whole-document validation;
* every reconciliation concludes with a passing Sprint Conformance Verification;
* the Engineering Evidence Package is complete;
* the Engineering Completion Report is complete; and
* execution stops at the authorized endpoint without commit, push, tag, or publication.

### Acceptance Criteria

Engineering Governance may accept the mission only when:

* scope compliance is demonstrated;
* the authority chain is coherent and non-conflicting across the reconciled records;
* all required decisions are traceably implemented or explicitly deferred;
* all validation and conformance results are reproducible and passing;
* repository integrity and unrelated working-tree changes are preserved;
* all required evidence and reporting are present; and
* no unauthorized activity occurred.

Engineering Governance alone determines mission acceptance.

## Phase Execution

### Phase 0 — Execution Contract and Baseline Verification

Purpose:

Establish deterministic authority and a reproducible campaign baseline.

Inputs:

* accepted EWO-000015 Revision 1;
* current repository-controlled governing records;
* current repository state;
* authoritative Engineering Governance decisions applicable to the campaign.

Activities:

* execute PROC-0001 Steps 1 through 4;
* verify EWO-000015 identifier, revision, Active lifecycle state, acceptance, uniqueness, and absence of a superseding Active revision;
* verify repository root, active branch, current commit, remote configuration, working tree, tools, access, and permissions;
* identify and preserve all pre-existing working-tree modifications;
* verify authoritative placement and discoverability through DOC-0001;
* verify the complete governing baseline and authorized document set;
* establish a traceable inventory of approved campaign decisions and explicit deferrals.

Expected Outputs:

* verified execution contract;
* operational inventory and preparation record;
* repository and working-tree baseline;
* governing-document inventory;
* approved-decision and deferral inventory.

Evidence Required:

* repository, branch, commit, remote, status, and integrity command results;
* Work Order metadata and uniqueness results;
* governing-record identities and revisions;
* decision authority references;
* record of pre-existing modifications.

Phase Completion Criteria:

Authority, scope, environment, baseline, and applicable decisions are complete, deterministic, and traceable.

Stop Conditions:

Stop if this Work Order has not been accepted, is not uniquely Active, has been superseded, is not discoverable, the governing baseline is incomplete, a decision lacks authoritative approval, repository integrity fails, or required preparation would exceed authority.

### Phase 1 — Complete-Document Discovery and Decision Inventory

Purpose:

Establish complete context before analysis or revision.

Inputs:

* completed Phase 0 outputs;
* all authorized controlled documents;
* governing and related controlled records;
* approved-decision and deferral inventory.

Activities:

For each authorized document, in the approved execution order:

* read the complete current revision before analysis;
* read every applicable governing record in full;
* establish document purpose, scope, authority, lifecycle state, metadata, relationships, requirements, and historical context;
* map every applicable approved decision to the complete publication;
* identify conflicts, omissions, duplications, ambiguity, and required reconciliation;
* confirm that the full proposed revision remains within authorized scope.

Expected Outputs:

* complete-document review record for each authorized document;
* decision-to-document traceability matrix;
* reconciliation requirements for each complete publication;
* findings requiring Engineering Governance action.

Evidence Required:

* identities and revisions of every document read;
* evidence of complete review;
* traceable decision sources;
* documented scope and conflict analysis.

Phase Completion Criteria:

Every document selected for revision has been reviewed in its entirety and possesses a complete, authority-backed reconciliation specification.

Stop Conditions:

Stop if complete review cannot be demonstrated, authority is ambiguous, decisions conflict, required content exceeds scope, or a proposed deferral lacks explicit Engineering Governance approval.

### Phase 2 — Complete Controlled Revisions

Purpose:

Produce each reconciled record as a coherent, complete engineering publication.

Inputs:

* completed Phase 1 outputs;
* current complete controlled document;
* approved reconciliation specification and decisions.

Activities:

For each authorized document:

* prepare the entire revised publication as one coherent controlled revision;
* preserve all valid existing concepts and historical integrity;
* incorporate every applicable approved decision not explicitly deferred;
* incorporate applicable newly approved decisions;
* reconcile metadata, authority statements, normative content, relationships, references, and revision history across the whole document;
* verify that no piecemeal editing or unauthorized content change occurred;
* do not begin the next document until the active document completes Phase 3.

Expected Outputs:

* a complete controlled revision for each authorized document;
* decision implementation traceability;
* change and scope evidence.

Evidence Required:

* complete revised publication;
* controlled diff against the preceding revision;
* mapping of changes to approved authority;
* proof that unrelated changes were preserved.

Phase Completion Criteria:

The active document exists as a complete, internally coherent controlled revision implementing all applicable non-deferred decisions within scope.

Stop Conditions:

Stop if a complete publication cannot be produced, an unapproved decision is required, unrelated content would be modified, historical integrity cannot be preserved, or the revision exceeds authority.

### Phase 3 — Whole-Document Validation and Sprint Conformance Verification

Purpose:

Prove that each complete controlled revision and the campaign remain conformant.

Inputs:

* complete controlled revision from Phase 2;
* governing baseline;
* approved reconciliation specification;
* repository and working-tree baseline.

Activities:

For each complete revised publication:

* validate YAML syntax and required metadata;
* validate document identifier, numbering, revision, lifecycle state, title, owner, classification, dates, and source-of-truth metadata;
* validate governing relationships, repository references, cross-references, and identifier uniqueness;
* validate complete-publication structure, internal consistency, normative language, and decision implementation;
* validate DOC-0001 discoverability and authoritative placement;
* validate repository integrity, scope compliance, unrelated-change preservation, and whitespace;
* perform Sprint Conformance Verification against this Work Order, the governing baseline, and approved campaign decisions;
* record all commands, results, exceptions, observations, and findings;
* return to Phase 1 for the next authorized document only after the active document passes.

Expected Outputs:

* whole-document validation results;
* Sprint Conformance Verification result for each reconciliation;
* traceable findings and recommendations;
* authorization to proceed to the next document or a stop report.

Evidence Required:

* reproducible validation commands and results;
* metadata, reference, uniqueness, and discovery results;
* scope and repository-integrity results;
* decision coverage results;
* explicit Sprint Conformance Verification outcome.

Phase Completion Criteria:

The active document passes every whole-document validation requirement and Sprint Conformance Verification, with no unresolved authority, scope, integrity, or decision-coverage defect.

Stop Conditions:

Stop on any validation failure, conformance failure, unresolved ambiguity, missing decision implementation, identifier conflict, discovery failure, repository-integrity failure, or unauthorized modification.

### Final Phase — Evidence and Completion Reporting

Purpose:

Provide the authoritative execution record required for Engineering Governance review and acceptance.

Inputs:

* all completed phase outputs;
* validation and Sprint Conformance Verification results;
* engineering findings and operational observations.

Activities:

* assemble the Engineering Evidence Package;
* produce the Engineering Completion Report;
* report scope, decision coverage, validation, conformance, files modified, runtime changes, repository integrity, findings, observations, and recommendations;
* leave Engineering Governance Notes blank;
* stop without commit, push, tag, publication, lifecycle transition, or follow-on reconciliation.

Expected Outputs:

* Engineering Evidence Package;
* Engineering Completion Report;
* final campaign status and recommendation.

Evidence Required:

* complete traceability from authority through execution and validation to each deliverable;
* final repository status and integrity results;
* complete list of modified files;
* confirmation that prohibited repository actions did not occur.

Phase Completion Criteria:

All required evidence and reporting are complete, reproducible, internally consistent, and ready for Engineering Governance review.

Stop Conditions:

Stop if evidence is incomplete, reporting cannot be reconciled to observed execution, an unresolved finding affects acceptance, or any further action requires Engineering Governance authorization.

## Validation Requirements

Every document reconciliation shall validate the complete revised publication against all applicable governing records and, at minimum, verify:

* YAML parsing;
* required metadata and lifecycle consistency;
* document numbering and identifier uniqueness;
* governing and related-document references;
* authoritative repository placement and DOC-0001 discoverability;
* complete-publication and template or architecture conformance, as applicable;
* internal authority-model consistency;
* implementation or explicit deferral of every applicable approved decision;
* whole-document semantic and normative consistency;
* preservation of historical integrity and unrelated working-tree modifications;
* repository integrity and whitespace;
* compliance with EWO-000015 scope and prohibitions; and
* Sprint Conformance Verification.

Validation evidence shall be objective, reproducible, attributable, and traceable. A failed requirement is a failed reconciliation and invokes a stop condition.

## Resume Policy

Upon interruption, the implementation agent shall:

1. verify the accepted, Active EWO-000015 revision and confirm that no newer Active revision supersedes it;
2. perform Operational Inventory;
3. perform Operational Preparation;
4. perform Baseline Verification, including comparison with the recorded working-tree baseline;
5. re-establish the approved decision and deferral inventory;
6. identify the first incomplete engineering phase and active document; and
7. resume only at that first incomplete phase.

A document reconciliation is complete only after its whole-document validation and Sprint Conformance Verification pass. Completed phases remain complete unless Engineering Governance authorizes repetition or baseline evidence demonstrates that their inputs changed.

## Stop Conditions

Execution shall stop immediately when:

* EWO-000015 has not been validated and accepted by Engineering Governance;
* authority is exceeded or additional Engineering Governance authorization is required;
* an applicable controlled document has not been reviewed in its entirety;
* an applicable approved decision is omitted without explicit deferral;
* governance authority, scope, or decision intent is ambiguous or conflicting;
* a complete controlled revision cannot be produced;
* piecemeal editing is detected;
* baseline or repository integrity is compromised;
* unrelated working-tree modifications cannot be preserved;
* deterministic execution or resume cannot be maintained;
* whole-document validation fails;
* Sprint Conformance Verification fails; or
* any Work Order-defined stop condition is encountered.

The implementation agent shall report the observation, evidence, mission impact, and recommendation. A Completion Report shall be produced before stopping whenever practical. No further engineering work shall occur until authorized.
